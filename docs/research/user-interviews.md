## Interview Guide 1: Clinic Director / Owner

**Target:** 5 interviews, 45 minutes each
**Goal:** Understand strategic pain points, buying criteria, and ROI expectations

### Pre-interview Screening
- Do you manage 3+ dental clinic branches?
- Are you the decision-maker for software purchases?
- What systems do you currently use for multi-branch management?

### Opening (5 min)
1. Tell me about your clinic network — how many branches, how many doctors/staff per branch, how long you've been operating?

### Current State — Multi-Branch Visibility (10 min)
2. When was the last time you needed to check performance across all branches? What did you do?
3. Walk me through how you currently track: (a) total appointments, (b) revenue per branch, (c) staff attendance.
4. How long does it take you to get those numbers right now?
5. What report do you check every Monday morning? Where does that data come from?

### Pain Points (10 min)
6. What's the biggest operational headache with running multiple branches?
7. Have you ever had a situation where Branch A was fully booked but Branch B had empty slots? How did you find out?
8. How do branch managers currently report to you? Excel? WhatsApp? Meeting?
9. What decision have you delayed because you didn't have the right data?

### Current Tools & Workarounds (10 min)
10. What software or tools do you use today for: booking, scheduling, inventory, reporting?
11. If you use Excel/Google Sheets — who maintains them? How often do they break or get out of sync?
12. Have you tried any clinic management software before? Why did you stop (or why didn't you start)?

### Buying Criteria (5 min)
13. If you could wave a magic wand and have one dashboard that shows you everything — what 5 numbers would you want to see?
14. Would you pay per-branch, per-user, or a flat monthly fee? What feels fair?
15. What would make you say "this is worth paying for" in the first demo?

### Closing (5 min)
16. If we built this — would you want all 4 modules (website, booking, operations, analytics) at once, or start with just booking?
17. Who else in your organization should I talk to? (branch manager, receptionist, doctor)

## Interview Guide 2: Branch Manager

**Target:** 5 interviews, 30 minutes each
**Goal:** Understand daily operational tasks and delegation pain points

### Opening (3 min)
1. How long have you been managing this branch? How many staff and doctors report to you?

### Daily Operations (10 min)
2. Walk me through your typical Monday morning — what's the first thing you check?
3. How do you create the weekly staff schedule? How far in advance?
4. When a staff member calls in sick, how do you handle coverage?
5. How do you know when you're running low on supplies (syringes, anesthesia, gloves)?

### Communication & Reporting (8 min)
6. How often do you report to the director? What do you send them?
7. What requests need director approval? (procurement, hiring, schedule changes) How do you submit those requests?
8. How do you communicate with receptionists and doctors — WhatsApp group? In-person?

### Pain Points (7 min)
9. What takes up the most time in your week that feels like it shouldn't?
10. Have you ever ordered supplies and realized too late that another branch already had extra stock?
11. What's the worst thing that can go wrong in a shift — and how do you prevent it?

### Closing (2 min)
12. If software could automate one part of your job, what would you pick?

## Interview Guide 3: Receptionist / Front Desk

**Target:** 5 interviews + 3 shadowing sessions
**Goal:** Understand booking flow, check-in process, and error-prone manual steps

### Opening (3 min)
1. How long have you worked at this clinic? Do you handle walk-ins only, or also phone/WA bookings?

### Booking Flow (12 min)
2. Walk me through what happens when a patient calls to book an appointment.
   - How do you check doctor availability?
   - How do you avoid double-booking?
   - What information do you collect from the patient?
3. Do patients book via WhatsApp? How do you handle that differently from phone calls?
4. How often do you get booking requests outside clinic hours (evenings, weekends)? What happens to those?
5. Have you ever double-booked a slot by accident? What caused it?

### Check-In & Daily Flow (8 min)
6. Show me how you track today's appointments — paper book? Excel? Other system?
7. When a patient arrives, what's the check-in process?
8. How do you handle no-shows? Do you call them? Mark them somehow?
9. If a doctor is running late, how do you inform waiting patients?

### Pain Points (5 min)
10. What's the most frustrating part of managing appointments?
11. Do patients cancel or reschedule often? How do you handle that?
12. If you could change one thing about the current booking process, what would it be?

### Closing (2 min)
13. Would you trust an online booking form where patients book directly, or do you prefer to confirm every booking manually?

## Shadowing Checklist: Receptionist Workflow

**Duration:** 2–3 hours during peak hours (morning or post-lunch)
**Method:** Observe, take notes, don't interrupt. Ask clarifying questions afterward.

### What to Observe
- [ ] How they check doctor schedules (paper calendar, phone, Excel, memory?)
- [ ] How they record a new booking (write down, type, verbal confirmation only?)
- [ ] What they ask patients (name, phone, insurance, reason for visit, specific doctor request?)
- [ ] How they handle conflicts (patient wants a full slot, doctor unavailable, insurance not accepted)
- [ ] Check-in process (patient arrives → how long until they're registered → what paperwork)
- [ ] No-show handling (do they mark it? call the patient? just leave the slot empty?)
- [ ] Inter-staff communication (how do they tell the doctor "patient is ready"?)
- [ ] Tools visible on desk (phone, WhatsApp on personal device or clinic device, notebook, computer)

### Post-Observation Questions
- How many bookings did you handle in that session? How many were walk-ins vs pre-booked?
- Did anything unusual happen today, or was this a typical morning?
- When was the last time the booking system (whatever it is) broke or caused confusion?

## Interview Guide 4: Doctor

**Target:** 5 interviews, 20 minutes each
**Goal:** Understand schedule preferences, availability communication, and patient history needs

### Opening (2 min)
1. How many years have you been practicing? Do you work at multiple branches or just one?

### Schedule & Availability (8 min)
2. How far in advance do you decide your weekly schedule?
3. If you need to block a day (personal leave, training, sick), how do you communicate that?
4. Do you ever feel overbooked? Or under-utilized?
5. Do patients request you specifically, or are they usually assigned by the receptionist?

### Patient Information (6 min)
6. Before a consultation, what information do you want to know about the patient?
7. Do you have access to their previous visit history? How do you access it?
8. If a patient has been to a different branch before, can you see that record?

### Pain Points (3 min)
9. What's the most annoying operational issue that disrupts your clinical work?
10. If you could change one thing about how appointments are scheduled for you, what would it be?

### Closing (1 min)
11. Would you use a web portal to manage your own availability, or do you prefer the receptionist to handle everything?

## Interview Guide 5: Patient

**Target:** 5 interviews, 15 minutes each
**Goal:** Understand discovery, booking behavior, and post-visit expectations

### Opening (2 min)
1. When was the last time you visited a dentist? Was it routine or emergency?

### Discovery (4 min)
2. How did you find that clinic? (Google search, referral, walked past it, insurance list?)
3. What information were you looking for before you decided to go? (price, doctor background, location, reviews?)
4. Did you visit their website or social media first, or just call/visit directly?

### Booking Experience (5 min)
5. How did you book your appointment? (walk-in, phone call, WhatsApp, online form?)
6. If you called or used WhatsApp — how long did it take to get a confirmed slot?
7. Did you receive a reminder before the appointment? (SMS, WA, call, none?)
8. Have you ever booked and then forgotten to go? What would have helped you remember?

### Visit Experience (3 min)
9. When you arrived, how long did you wait before being seen?
10. Did you feel like the staff knew why you were there, or did you have to re-explain everything?

### Closing (1 min)
11. If you could book your next dental appointment as easily as ordering food online, would you prefer that or still call?
